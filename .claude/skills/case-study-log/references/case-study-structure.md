# Common Components of a successful case study entry:
- Names a concrete number (time, %, $, count etc.) — not "it was slow," but "requests hung for ~10.5 seconds", quantitative metrics
- States the root cause, not just the symptom
- States the actual decision made, and against what alternative- the rationality being the decision 
- States the measurable impact of the fix
- Includes visuals if necessary
